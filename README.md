# Longboard strap snap clip

On Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7415314 · Source: https://github.com/korjavin/skateholder

Snap-in clip that lives on a carry strap and clicks into the arch-shaped hole at the nose/tail of a
drop-through longboard (made for a Decathlon/Oxelo drop-through deck). Print two, one per end.

Current version: `stl/snap_clip_v4.stl`. Photos of the deck/hole: `photos/`.

## How it works

- Top plate with two slots: a 25 mm webbing strap threads through like a tri-glide, no sewing.
- Two spring legs go through the deck hole; the barbs click under the deck.
- To remove: squeeze both barbs from below (like a backpack buckle) and pull up.

## Fit (v4)

- Hole: 27 mm wide, semicircular end, ~15 mm from the truck plate edge to the top of the arch.
- Barb catch face 14.3 mm below the top plate (deck ~14–15 mm incl. grip), barb reach 3.2 mm.

## Printing

- Material: PETG (PA/nylon even better). Not PLA: it cracks on the legs and softens in the sun.
- Orientation: lay it on the flat back face so the legs flex along the layers. No supports.
- 6+ walls, 100% infill, nozzle at the high end of your PETG range.

## Versions

- v2: barb 2.2 mm, catch face 15.3 mm.
- v3: barb 7.2 mm, legs −5 mm — too stiff, don't print.
- v4: barb 3.2 mm, catch face 14.3 mm — recommended.
